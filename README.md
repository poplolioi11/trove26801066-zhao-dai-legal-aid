# 兩善俱備（《東華報》1906-08-25 第6版，澳洲國家圖書館） / Liang shan ju bei [A Community Fund for Bereaved Families and Legal Aid]: Transcription Dataset

《東華報》1906-08-25 刊載的〈兩善俱備〉。報載雪梨華商共同擬議兩項捐款。一項撫恤趙帶案死者家屬，另一項為趙帶延請狀師，並附對精神失常與責任的報館評論。同版另有一篇案件記事，兩者各有獨立題頭。

Liang shan ju bei [A Community Fund for Bereaved Families and Legal Aid] appeared in Tung Wah Times (1906-08-25). The newspaper describes a Sydney Chinese merchants' meeting to raise money both for bereaved families and for the legal defence of Zhao Dai, with editorial comment on mental disorder and responsibility. It is separately headed from the report on the same case printed on the same page.

## V0.2.0

完整收錄第6版此篇原題與正文 13 個原欄，全文格式為 TXT、JSON 與 TEI XML。比對獨立原圖讀法後回圖裁定，採用 4 筆訂正，留存疑讀定位。

TXT, JSON and TEI XML cover the complete separately headed article, comprising the heading and 13 source columns on page 6. An independent image reading was compared with the first transcription. Source-image adjudication adopted 4 changes and retained unresolved readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。照錄原題、時代用字與原欄次。疑讀按欄記在 JSON。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Headings, historical terminology and source column order are preserved. Uncertainties are recorded by column. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
